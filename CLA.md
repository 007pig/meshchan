## 9. How to accept

Before merge, each rights holder must post its own explicit acceptance comment
on the relevant pull request from the contributor's authenticated GitHub account.
An authorized representative may accept for a named organization. Use the
following template, replacing every bracketed field with an actual value:

```text
I have read and accept the Meshchan Contributor License Agreement version 1.0
(meshchan-cla-1.0).

CLA document: https://github.com/007pig/meshchan/blob/[full-commit-SHA]/CLA.md
CLA SHA-256: [SHA-256 of the exact CLA.md at that commit]
Contributor GitHub account: @[account]
Capacity: [individual rights holder OR authorized representative of organization]
Organization: [organization name OR not applicable]
Contribution pull request: https://github.com/007pig/meshchan/pull/[number]
Covered head commit: [full head commit SHA]
Third-party material and exclusions: [none OR exact paths, sources, and licenses]

I retain copyright in my Contribution and grant 007pig the permissions in this
agreement, including use in the GPL-3.0-only community edition and paid,
closed-source commercial editions with the corresponding sublicensing rights.
I confirm that I am entitled to grant those permissions for the covered work.
I intend this posted statement to record my electronic acceptance.
```

Use a commit containing this version of the CLA, such as the current base-branch
commit after this policy is merged. A branch name, "latest," or an unfilled
template is not an immutable agreement reference. Obtain the file hash from the
exact referenced commit, not from a different working-tree copy.

Posting a pull request, checking a box, a bot comment, a commit's author field,
or a `Signed-off-by` line alone is **not** acceptance of this CLA. If the head
changes before merge, post a new complete acceptance comment for the new head.
Every coauthor's relevant rights must be covered before merge.

The maintainer must verify the account, scope, agreement text/hash, signing
capacity, and disclosed exclusions. Before merge, preserve the full acceptance
comment, author and comment URL/ID, posting time, accepted CLA bytes/version/hash,
covered commits, and the review outcome in a durable project record. Retain
necessary confidential authority evidence privately, not in public comments.
Do not request home addresses, identity documents, signatures containing private
information, or secrets in a public pull request.

If public electronic acceptance is unsuitable or authority cannot be verified,
leave the contribution unmerged until 007pig and the rights holder agree on an
appropriate recorded acceptance process for the same exact terms and scope.
007pig, or an expressly authorized representative identified in the record,
must acknowledge acceptance of the Contribution under the identified CLA,
recording the contributor comment, agreement version/hash, covered head, and
reviewed exclusions before merge. This acknowledgement records the Recipient's
acceptance of its commitments; a bot or checkbox does not provide it.
This repository does not currently run an automated CLA-signing service; this
is an explicit, manual review requirement.
